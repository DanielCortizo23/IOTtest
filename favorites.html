My 3 Favorite Moments & Plays

1. The 6-Ace Masterclass vs Canada (2019)

Why I chose this: It showcases ice-cold mental composure under pressure. Closing out a crucial tiebreaker set completely by yourself with unreturnable serves is something that rarely happens in high-level volleyball.

2. Back-Row Pipe Attack Against Triple Blocks

Why I chose this: Despite opposing blockers often standing over 2.05 m tall, Nishida's hangtime and sharp wrist snap allow him to bounce the ball straight inside the 3-meter line right over their hands.

3. Infectious Energy and Court Celebrations

Why I chose this: Beyond technical skill, Nishida plays with pure passion. His screams, high-fives, and emotional leadership hype up his teammates and make watching volleyball an unforgettable experience.
